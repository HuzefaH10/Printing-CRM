# PRINTING BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM
## DATA PACK 2 — UI/UX BLUEPRINT
### Layouts · Navigation · Dashboards · Micro-interactions

---

## 2.1 UI/UX CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Density over Decoration:** The application is a workspace, not a marketing site. It should display a high density of information without feeling cluttered. We prioritize data visibility over large amounts of white space.
2. **The "Three-Click Maximum" Rule:** Any specific job, client, or quote must be reachable in three clicks or fewer from anywhere in the app.
3. **Keyboard First (Speed of Capture):** Adding a note, logging a call, or starting a new quote should have global keyboard shortcuts. You shouldn't have to navigate away from your current screen to log a thought.
4. **Contextual Awareness:** A user should never look at a "Job" without seeing the "Client" context adjacent to it (e.g., past jobs, recent complaints, total lifetime value).

---

## 2.2 GLOBAL LAYOUT

The app will follow a **fixed sidebar + topbar layout**, standard for modern enterprise SaaS, but highly optimized for speed.

### A. The Left Sidebar (Main Navigation)
Persistent, collapsible to icons to save screen real estate.
- **Dashboard / Command Center** (Home)
- **Organizations** (Clients & Prospects)
- **Pipeline** (Opportunities & Quotes)
- **Production** (Active Jobs & Tracking)
- **Tenders** (Public/Private bids)
- **Settings**

### B. The Topbar (Global Action & Search)
- **Global Omni-Search Bar (Cmd/Ctrl + K):** Searches across all entities instantly. Typing "KOC" brings up the KOC Organization, KOC Contacts, recent KOC Quotes, and active KOC Jobs in a unified dropdown.
- **Global "Quick Add" Button (+):** Allows instant creation of: New Contact, New Opportunity, New Note. If you are on an Organization page, clicking "Quick Add Note" automatically links the note to that organization.
- **Notification Bell / Active Alerts:** Highlights proactive system alerts (e.g., "KOC Annual Report due for re-quote in 14 days").

## 2.3 THE COMMAND CENTER (Dashboard)

The Dashboard is not just a chart page; it is an active to-do list generated by the system's intelligence.

**Section 1: "Needs Attention" (Top Priority)**
- *Actionable System Alerts:* 
  - "Ministry of Health has been dormant for 6 months. Send a check-in?"
  - "Quote for 'University Prospectus' sent 48 hours ago. Follow up today."
  - "Production deadline for JOB-0042 is tomorrow, status is still 'Prepress'."

**Section 2: Pipeline Snapshot (Middle Left)**
- Active Quotes grouped by status (Scoping, Quoted, Negotiating).
- Total expected value closing this week.

**Section 3: Production Floor (Middle Right)**
- Mini-kanban of jobs currently running: Prepress, Printing, Finishing, Out for Delivery.
- Quick visual flags (Red/Yellow/Green) for jobs approaching deadlines.

---

## 2.4 THE "360° ORGANIZATION" VIEW

This is the most important screen in the application. It breaks the traditional separation of Sales, CRM, and Production.

**Left Column (Static Data & Metrics):**
- Company Name, Status, Industry.
- Key Financials: Lifetime Value, Average Margin, Win Rate.
- Primary Contacts list.

**Main Center Column (The Timeline):**
- A unified chronological feed of *everything* that has happened with this client.
- E.g., An email sent (today) -> A job delivered (last week) -> A quote lost (last month) -> A meeting note (3 months ago).
- Filters at the top to isolate: Just Emails, Just Jobs, Just Quotes.

**Right Column (Active Context):**
- Currently Active Quotes (if any).
- Currently Running Jobs (if any).
- Next scheduled follow-up.

## 2.5 THE OPPORTUNITY & JOB BOARDS

### The Pipeline (Opportunities)
- **Default View:** Kanban Board.
- **Columns:** Lead → Scoping → Quoted → Negotiation → Won/Lost.
- Drag-and-drop to move a quote through the pipeline.
- Dropping a card into "Won" triggers a modal: "Create Job from this Quote?"

### The Production Floor (Jobs)
- **Default View:** Table/List view (high density). Kanban is optional but table is better for production tracking.
- **Columns:** Job Number, Client, Description, Status, Specs Summary (e.g., "5000x A4, 300gsm"), Delivery Deadline.
- Clicking a Job row opens a **Slide-out Panel (Drawer)** rather than navigating to a new page, keeping the user in the context of the whole production floor.

---

## 2.6 MICRO-INTERACTIONS & EFFICIENCY

1. **The Slide-Out Panel:** When viewing lists (jobs, quotes, people), clicking an item slides out a drawer from the right side of the screen containing the details, rather than loading a new page. This maintains context and allows fast multi-tasking.
2. **Instant Field Editing:** On the 360° View, clicking a status or a phone number immediately turns it into an editable text field (inline editing). No "Edit Mode" required.
3. **Smart Defaults:** If a user creates a new Quote for a client who only ever orders "Saddle-Stitch A5 Booklets", the system pre-fills those specs as a suggestion.
4. **Frictionless Logging:** Pressing `N` anywhere in the app opens a "Quick Note" floating window to jot down a phone call without losing your place.

---

## 2.7 AESTHETICS & DESIGN LANGUAGE

- **Color Palette:** 
  - Background: Clean, stark White (`#FFFFFF`) or very light Gray (`#F9FAFB`) to reduce glare.
  - Sidebar: Deep Charcoal (`#1F2937`) or Navy for strong visual separation.
  - Accents: Subdued Blue (`#2563EB`) for primary actions.
  - Status Colors (Crucial for scanning): Green for Won/Delivered, Amber for Pending/Warning, Red for Lost/Late, Gray for Dormant.
- **Typography:** 
  - "Inter" or "Roboto" - Highly legible sans-serif fonts optimized for dense data tables.
